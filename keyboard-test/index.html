This needs a physical keyboard On a touch screen there are no key events to read. Connect a keyboard, or open this page on a computer.
Some keys never reach the browser Fn, media keys and anything your keyboard handles in its own firmware are invisible to any web page. They are not broken — they simply never arrive.
A few shortcuts belong to the browser F5, F11, F12 and Ctrl/Cmd with W, T, N or R stay with the browser so you are never trapped on this page. Everything else is held back while Hold shortcuts is on.
Keys at once Hold several keys together to see how many your keyboard can report simultaneously. Cheaper boards stop at around six, or drop keys in certain combinations.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
