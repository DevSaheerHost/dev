# Google Business Profile — S-Core Tech

Set this up before you do anything else to the website.

For the repair half of the business this is worth more than the site. Someone
with a cracked screen opens Maps or types "mobile repair near me". They tap a
listing with photos and reviews. They almost never scroll to a website. The
site's job is to close someone who already found you; **this is how they find
you.**

It is free and takes one evening. Start at <https://business.google.com>.

---

## The fields, filled in

**Business name**

> S-Core Tech

Just that. Not "S-Core Tech — Best Laptop Repair Malappuram". Stuffing
keywords into the name is against Google's guidelines and is the most common
reason a listing gets suspended. The name is also how people will ask for you
out loud.

**Primary category**

> Computer repair service

Pick this one, not "Electronics store". The primary category decides which
searches you appear in more than anything else you will ever type. If most of
your money comes from phones rather than laptops, use **Mobile phone repair
shop** instead — choose by where the revenue actually is, not by what sounds
bigger.

**Additional categories**

> Mobile phone repair shop
> Computer service
> Website designer

Add "Website designer" last. It is real, but it should not outrank the repair
categories, and a listing that claims too many unrelated things ranks worse,
not better.

**Service area**

Set this as a service area business **as well as** an address, because you do
doorstep pickup. List the towns you will actually travel to:

> Malappuram, Manjeri, Perinthalmanna, Tirur, Kottakkal

Only list places you will really go. A pickup you cancel costs you a review.

**Hours**

Put your real hours, including the Friday afternoon break if you take one.
Google shows "Closed" in red at the exact moment someone needs you, and being
wrong about it is worse than being closed.

**Phone**

> +91 75929 49476

The same number as the site. One number everywhere.

**Website**

Your domain once you have one. Until then, leave it blank rather than pointing
at `saheerlab.vercel.app/s-core-tech/` — a long URL on a lab subdomain reads
as "not a real business" to exactly the customer you are trying to win.

Paste this:

>

>

That is 712 characters. The first sentence is the one that gets read.

## Photos — the part that decides it

This matters more than the words. Listings with real photos get roughly double
the clicks of listings without, and almost every repair shop uploads either
nothing or one blurry photo of a shutter.

Upload at least:

- **The shopfront**, in daylight, so people recognise it from the road.
- **The bench**: soldering station, microscope, boards laid out. This is the
  photo that separates you from the man who only swaps screens.
- **Four or five repairs in progress.** A board under the microscope. A screen
  half-fitted. Hands doing the work.
- **You.** One clear photo of the person who will answer the phone. Local
  trade runs on faces.
- **A logo** for the profile picture and a wide shot for the cover.

Same rule as the site's proof folder: nothing identifying in frame — no lock
screens, no contacts, no repair tags with customer numbers.

Add two or three new photos a month. Google treats an active listing as a live
business and a dormant one as a dead one.

## Reviews

This is the whole game, and it is the one thing I cannot build for you.

- Ask **every** customer, at the moment you hand the device back working and
  they are pleased. Not by message the next day.
- Get the short link from your dashboard (Ask for reviews → copy link) and
  send that. Do not ask them to search for you; they will not.
- Never pay for reviews, never ask staff or family to post them, never offer a
  discount for one. Google detects clusters and it costs you the listing.
- **Reply to every review**, including the bad ones. Reply to a bad one
  calmly, factually, and offer to fix it. People read the reply more carefully
  than the complaint, and a good reply to a one-star does more for you than
  another five-star.

Ten honest reviews puts you ahead of most of your competition locally.

## Posts

Post once a week. It takes two minutes and keeps the listing active:

- A repair you finished, with a photo and the turnaround ("Dead MacBook,
  liquid damage, back with the owner in 24 hours").
- An offer ("Free pickup across Manjeri this week").
- Something from the web side ("Built a QR menu for a cafe — customers order
  on WhatsApp without downloading anything").

---

## After it is live

1. Make sure the name, address and phone number are **byte-identical** on the
   listing, the website footer and anywhere else you are listed. Mismatched
   details split your ranking.
2. Add the same details to JustDial and IndiaMART — your customers use them.
3. Once the domain exists, put it on the listing and add the Google review
   link to the website footer.

Check the listing's own stats after a month: it tells you how many people
called, asked for directions, and tapped the website. That is your real
scoreboard, not the site's analytics.
